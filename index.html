BigBrainClipz

BigBrainClipz publishes short-form clips (YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok videos) made from footage that brands and creators provide for official clipping campaigns.

Clipping Agent is the private tool BigBrainClipz uses to manage this work. It runs locally on the owner's computer and uses the official APIs of YouTube, Instagram and TikTok to upload videos to the BigBrainClipz accounts and to read basic statistics (such as view counts) of those videos. It is not offered to other users.
