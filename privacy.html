Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Clipping Agent ("the app") is a private tool operated by BigBrainClipz. It is used only by its owner to manage the BigBrainClipz accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): through the YouTube Data API, the app uploads videos to the owner's channel and reads the channel's basic information (channel ID, handle, title, subscriber count) and statistics of videos it uploaded (view counts).
  • Instagram (Meta): through the Instagram API, the app reads the account's basic profile (user ID, username, account type, follower count), publishes Reels, and reads insights (views) of media it published.
  • TikTok: through the TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API, the app reads basic profile information (open ID, username, display name, follower count), posts videos, checks posting status and reads view counts of its videos.

How data is used

The data is used only to publish the owner's videos, to confirm that the correct account is connected, and to show the owner how the videos perform. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Storage and security

The app runs on the owner's own computer. OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in the operating system's encrypted keychain (macOS Keychain), never in plain files. Account metadata and video statistics are stored in a local database on the same computer. No data is stored on external servers operated by the app.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when an account is disconnected in the app. Access can also be revoked at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, Instagram under Settings → Website permissions, TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. After revocation, stored data related to that account is deleted within 30 days, or earlier on request.
